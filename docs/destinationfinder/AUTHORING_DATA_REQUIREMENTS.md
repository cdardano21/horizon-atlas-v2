# DestinationFinder Authoring Data Requirements

## Purpose and authority

This is the per-destination research and authoring contract for every future DestinationFinder workbook batch. It answers one operational question: **what exact information must be gathered for each destination before the destination is authoring-complete?**

Authority, from strongest to weakest:

1. `app/lib/workbook-v31-deterministic-core.ts` (actual parse and canonical construction)
2. `app/lib/intelligence-v2/workbook-v32-adapter.ts` and `workbook-v32-normalization.ts` (runtime facts and controlled values)
3. Smart Shortlist evaluator, server-data loader, and owned-affordability evaluator
4. Executable acceptance tests, especially `next-batch-20-cleanup-workbook.test.ts`
5. The authorized v3.3 Next-20 workbook, including `VALIDATION_RULES` and `PREMIUM_REQUIREMENTS`
6. Design documents; code wins when they drift

The reference workbook contains 48 sheets. The parser hard-requires these ten structural sheets: `DESTINATIONS`, `DESTINATION_FACTS`, `DESTINATION_SCORES`, `IMPORT_CONTRACT`, `PILOT_STATUS`, `WORKBOOK_METADATA`, `IMPORT_MANIFEST`, `DESTINATION_ALIASES`, `VALIDATION_RULES`, and `DATA_DICTIONARY`. Most content sheets may be empty and still parse. **Schema-valid is not authoring-complete.** A destination must pass both the structural/runtime contract and the customer-content parity contract below.

## Requirement vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **REQUIRED** | Every destination must provide a valid value or row. Blank and `UNKNOWN` are not allowed unless the row explicitly says otherwise. |
| **CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED** | Required when the condition applies, such as a coastal destination, a legal pathway, or a locally available category. If it does not apply, record the supported negative/`NONE` value where a controlled field exists. |
| **RECOMMENDED** | Does not block authoring-complete, but belongs in a production-quality destination page. |
| **OPTIONAL** | Add only when reliable, destination-specific evidence exists. Never fabricate it to fill a sheet. |

`UNKNOWN` means “not established by admissible evidence,” not “No,” zero, blank, or “probably.” Where a field has no controlled `UNKNOWN` token, leave the field blank and explain the gap in `notes`, `evidence_summary`, or the closest narrative field. An intentionally unsupported row is omitted; it is never represented by a zero score.

## MINIMUM DATASET REQUIRED BEFORE A DESTINATION IS AUTHORING-COMPLETE

All items below are mandatory even though the parser accepts less.

| Minimum deliverable per destination | Exact workbook location | Acceptance rule |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical identity and page shell | `DESTINATIONS` | All REQUIRED identity/editorial fields in the identity table below; exact unique key/slug; `enabled=TRUE`; publication state intentionally set. |
| Batch ownership and operation | `IMPORT_MANIFEST`, `DESTINATION_ALIASES`, `PILOT_STATUS`; external expansion registry | Exactly one manifest row and canonical alias row; expected key is registry-owned; workbook SHA-256 is pinned before activation. |
| Smart Shortlist affordability | `COST_OF_LIVING` | Exactly two `u3_r5_total_monthly_estimate` rows: one `single`, one `couple`; `lifestyle_tier=comfortable`; `stay_mode_key=RELOCATE`; 2026 USD; finite positive bounds with high >= low; source and verification complete. |
| Every current hard-gate input | `DESTINATIONS`, `VISA_RESIDENCY`, `HOUSING_PROPERTY`, `HEALTHCARE_INSURANCE`, `SAFETY_RISKS`, `LGBTQ_INCLUSIVITY`, `LIFESTYLE_FEATURES` | Every field in the hard-gate table is explicitly researched and encoded with a valid known value or explicit `UNKNOWN`. UNKNOWN is allowed for honest runtime behavior but **blocks authoring-complete** unless the table explicitly says otherwise. |
| Overview | `DESTINATIONS.short_description`, `DESTINATIONS.long_description` | One concise relocation/lifestyle summary and one place-specific 400-600+ word overview covering cost, climate, housing, healthcare, and mobility. |
| Facts/evidence backbone | `DESTINATION_FACTS`, `SOURCES` | Key facts needed by derived climate/page copy are destination-scoped and sourced. At least 10 distinct source records; target 20. Major claims trace to primary/credible sources. |
| Neighborhoods | `NEIGHBORHOODS` | At least 5 actual locally meaningful areas, target 8; required descriptive fields, map URL, source, and verification. Tiny destinations may document fewer only with an explicit reason in batch notes. |
| Named places | `PLACES` | Meet the applicable minimums in the places table. Every place is real, current, mapped, sourced, and useful to a resident/relocator. |
| Practical links | `RESOURCES`, `PROPERTY_RESOURCES` | Meet applicable resource/property-link minimums; links resolve and descriptions explain their use. |
| Media | `MEDIA` | At least 3 verified destination-specific images, target 5; exactly one primary image; usable source/credit/license record; no uncertain identity. |
| Cost and climate | `COST_OF_LIVING`, `CLIMATE_MONTHLY` | Required U3-R5 rows plus 12 months of high/low/rainfall and humidity where reliably available. Preserve climate formulas and recalculate cached values. |
| Relocation modules | Content modules from `HOUSING_PROPERTY` through `REALITY_CHECK` | Meet each **REQUIRED** row below, including one healthcare-system, visa/long-stay, tax, LGBTQ, language, accessibility, work/business, retirement, and practical reality record. |
| Reality, not promotion | `SAFETY_RISKS`, `REALITY_CHECK` | At least 3 safety/risk rows (target 5) and 5 reality-check rows (target 6), with specific mitigations/evidence and no tourism-board gloss. |
| Population parity | `DESTINATIONS.population`, `SOURCES` | Positive numeric population for every destination plus a destination-scoped credible source/provenance row. `metro_population` is optional and must be added only where meaningful and supported. |
| Lifestyle page parity | `LIFESTYLE_FEATURES` | Target minimum 14 displayable, evidence-backed, sourced, destination-specific rows representing multiple meaningful themes. Richer destinations may exceed 14. A legitimate inability to reach 14 requires explicit documented review; never pad with irrelevant or duplicated filler. |
| Human-readable customer copy | All customer-visible fields | Copy describes the destination, not the data pipeline. Clearly internal terms are blocking; ambiguous ordinary-English uses require review. |

A destination is **not** authoring-complete if a critical field is merely blank, if a hard-gate value is still `UNKNOWN`, if source provenance is missing, if formula results are stale/unavailable, or if minimum counts are met by duplicated, generic, invented, or irrelevant rows.

A premium/full destination should meet the target counts below, populate every applicable RECOMMENDED field, and add optional modules only where evidence supports them.

| Health/mobility | 8 hospital/urgent-care places, 3 healthcare/insurance resources, 5 local-mobility rows, and 3 airport/global-access rows. |

| Digital/practical | 5 connectivity/coworking resources/records; complete daily-life and environmental modules. |
| Reality/seasonality | 5 safety rows, 6 reality checks, 12 monthly climate rows, and useful event/season records. |
| Media | 5+ verified/licensed photos, 3 useful social-media resources, and 1 reliable webcam where available. |

## Places and local categories

For every authored `PLACES` row, `place_key`, `destination_key`, `category_key`, `place_name`, `subcategory`, `description`, `best_for`, `display_order`, `source_name`, `source_url`, `google_maps_url`, `verified`, and `confidence` are **REQUIRED**. `website_url` is **REQUIRED** when an official site exists; `address` is **REQUIRED** for fixed-location businesses/facilities; `neighborhood_key`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `price_level`, `phone`, and `verified_at` are **RECOMMENDED**. UNKNOWN is represented by a blank optional field, never invented content. Place data serves destination pages/import persistence, not current hard gates. A valid row names a real facility, explains why it matters, links its official site and exact map, and cites a checked source. Common failures are stale/closed venues, category stuffing, map search URLs, cross-destination places, unsupported coordinates, and generic descriptions.

Allowed `PLACES.category_key` values are exactly:

| Parent | Exact categories | Authoring requirement and applicable minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Food/drink | `restaurant`, `coffee_shop`, `bakery`, `brewery_winery` | Restaurants **REQUIRED** min 8/target 10; coffee min 5/target 8; bakery min 3/target 5. `brewery_winery` **CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED** where locally material. |
| Daily life | `grocery`, `shopping`, `farmers_market` | Grocery **REQUIRED** min 4/target 8; shopping min 4/target 8; farmers markets **CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED** where available. |
| Outdoors | `park`, `trail`, `golf`, `pickleball_tennis`, `water_recreation`, `skiing_winter`, `beach` | Parks **REQUIRED** min 5/target 8. At least 3 relevant trail/water/ski rows. Golf min 3 where supply supports it. Beach required for `DIRECT_ACCESS`/`NEARBY`; ski required for `SKI_RESORT_ACCESS`. Other categories conditional on local availability. |
| Wellness/health | `gym`, `hospital`, `urgent_care`, `pharmacy` | At least 4 combined hospital/urgent-care rows, target 8. Pharmacy and gym **RECOMMENDED**. |
| Culture | `attraction`, `museum`, `live_music`, `nightlife`, `theater`, `sports`, `zoo_aquarium` | At least 8 combined culture/attraction rows, target 15. Nightlife min 3 where relevant; other categories conditional on local supply. |
| Work/community | `coworking`, `religious_community`, `lgbtq_resource`, `social_club` | Coworking **CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED** for a remote-work proposition; others **RECOMMENDED** where real resources exist. |
| Pets/family/aging/safety | `pet_service`, `vet`, `emergency_vet`, `school`, `international_school`, `childcare`, `senior_living`, `emergency_service` | **CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED** when the corresponding module claims availability; include named evidence for material services. |
| Mobility | `transit_hub` | **CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED** for major rail/ferry/bus access described in transport records. |

Do not invent extra category keys such as `hotel`, `marina`, `lake`, or `ski_resort`. Hotels belong in `RESOURCES.resource_category=hotels`; marinas and lakes use `water_recreation` with a precise `subcategory`; ski resorts use `skiing_winter`.

## Customer-copy gate

Customer-visible text must describe the destination, not the workbook or implementation. Clearly internal phrases such as `Intelligence V2`, parser, adapter, enum, source field, hard gate, preference fit, normalization, runtime, current engine/contract, schema row, `DESTINATIONS.<field>`, or uppercase schema names such as `PLACES`/`RESOURCES` are blocking. Potentially ordinary words such as workbook, canonical, database, import, and token trigger review when context is ambiguous. Internal documentation sheets are outside this copy gate. Never evade the gate with a generic sanitizer; correct the source authoring while preserving verified meaning.

## Research and workbook completion checklist

Use this sequence for **each destination**. A future ChatGPT batch-authoring task should not proceed by guessing beyond it.

1. Reserve the exact `destination_key`, slug, canonical name, and ISO country code; confirm the key is owned by the authorized batch registry entry.
2. Complete `DESTINATIONS`, including publication state, coordinates, currency/language/time zone, descriptions, and exact beach/mountain enums.
3. Research every hard-gate row in the complete hard-gate table from primary sources. Encode known `Yes`/`No`/enum values; use `Unknown` only when evidence truly cannot establish the answer, and flag the destination as not authoring-complete.
4. Build exactly one valid `single` and one valid `couple` U3-R5 2026 USD rollup. Document assumptions, sources, and verification dates; independently recalculate both midpoints.
5. Gather climate source facts and confirm all 12 `CLIMATE_MONTHLY` rows. Preserve formulas in columns C-F, recalculate workbook caches, and inspect output values for destination-row misalignment.
6. Write the short and long overview only after cost, climate, housing, healthcare, safety, and transport evidence exists. Every material claim must trace to a module source.
7. Meet minimum neighborhood, place, resource, property-resource, media, safety, reality-check, and move-checklist counts with distinct, current, destination-specific records.
8. Complete healthcare, visa, tax, LGBTQ, language, family, accessibility, setup, work, retirement, transport, connectivity, and other applicable module rows. A blank sheet is not proof that the topic does not apply.
9. Add at least 10 source-catalog rows and link critical facts to official/primary evidence. Verify URLs, claims, and dates. Review media identity and usage rights separately.
10. Do not author `DESTINATION_SCORES` unless an approved, documented 0-100 methodology supports the exact canonical key. Omit unsupported dimensions.
11. Reconcile actual row counts with `IMPORT_MANIFEST`; validate unique keys, foreign keys, same-destination neighborhood references, one primary image, and no cross-destination content.
12. Confirm all required `verified_at` dates, inspect every explicit `UNKNOWN`, and keep the destination out of authoring-complete status until critical unknowns are resolved.
13. Recalculate/save the XLSX, load it through the deterministic parser, require zero validation errors, and inspect the canonical destination for retained rows and cached formula values.
14. Validate exact registry key ownership and pinned SHA-256, run the workbook acceptance tests, and verify an identical reimport produces no content diff before activation.

## Non-negotiable failure conditions

- A destination key, child key, or alias is blank, duplicated, fuzzy-matched, cross-destination, or outside registry ownership.
- Either U3-R5 household row is missing, duplicated, non-USD, nonnumeric, nonpositive, inverted, stale, or unsourced.
- A current hard-gate field is blank, invalidly tokenized, unsupported by evidence, or represented only in prose that the adapter does not consume.
- A verified critical row lacks its supporting URL/name or verification date where the sheet provides one.
- A source is irrelevant to the destination/claim, a listing/aggregator is misrepresented as official, or an invalid URL was replaced rather than flagged.
- Climate formulas were overwritten, point at another destination's fact rows, or have no recalculated cached values.
- A direct preference score was guessed, assigned zero for missing evidence, authored under an unsupported key, or lacks methodology/evidence.
- Minimum counts are satisfied with generic, duplicated, invented, closed, or geographically wrong content.
- Media identity, license, attribution, or current availability is not established.
- The manifest counts/status do not match the authored destination, the workbook hash is unpinned, or deterministic import reports any validation error.
